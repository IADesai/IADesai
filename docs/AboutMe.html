Education

I am a Databricks-certified data engineer and a first-class honours chemical engineering graduate from University College London, with a minor in programming. I have built my experience from the ground up: through my degree, self-directed learning, intensive courses, certifications, hackathons, and hands-on industry work.

Coding has always been my creative outlet. Since the age of eight, when I discovered the pleasure of coding via Scratch, I have never stopped learning and exploring. My academic path took me through the pure sciences and into chemical engineering, but my passion for programming persisted, leading me to a minor in programming with modules in Application Programming for Data Science and Applied Software Engineering. Engineering taught me how physical systems move and transform material; data engineering lets me apply the same thinking to information.

Experience

My grounding in data came at Sigma Labs, where I trained as a software and data engineer across backend development, data science, data engineering and cloud computing, culminating in four technical case studies. One of those, a media sentiment pipeline, has since grown into Coup, which I still lead today.

Most recently, at Mobiess (part of Bellrock Group), I worked as a Software Onboarding Consultant and Technical Support Specialist. Within my first two months I spearheaded an AI Survey Creator using agentic development, prompt engineering and MCP servers. I also built automated reports and dashboards for audit assurance, used SQL to get to the bottom of surveyor bottlenecks, and helped increase survey efficiency by up to 20%. Alongside that, I have worked as an IT engineer building the infrastructure for an accounting business, and as a freelance web developer building sites that serve thousands of users a month.

My street smarts came earlier, and the hard way. As Managing Director of H&C Retailing, I took a halal Chinese steamed chicken stall from an idea to a business on Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Hungry Panda. Between that, working the door as an SIA-licensed supervisor and pulling shots as a barista, I learned to read a queue, stay calm when everything arrives at once, and get the order right the first time. It turns out that is not far off running a data pipeline.

Tech stack

Python and SQL are my mainstays, from PostgreSQL and SQLite to Spark SQL. On the data engineering side I work with Databricks, Spark, Kafka, ETL and stream processing, and I deploy to the cloud with AWS, Terraform and Docker. I visualise data with Tableau, Plotly, Dash and Streamlit, and on the AI side I build with MCP servers, multi-agent frameworks and prompt engineering. Clean code, TDD and Git hold it all together.

What drives me

I am most interested in data infrastructure: data centres, and the large-scale movement and storage of information. I learn best by getting into the nitty-gritty day to day, understanding how systems run in practice, how to make them more efficient, and how new technologies can improve performance and reduce costs. Over time I want to help architect systems, shape procedures, and help teams make well-informed technical decisions. I welcome feedback, and I am always happy to learn from people who have been doing this longer than I have.
